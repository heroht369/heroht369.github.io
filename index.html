Frequently asked questions

How do I apply a filter or style to a photo?

Open iStyles, import a photo from your camera or library, then scroll the filter tray at the bottom and tap any style to preview it instantly. Tap the download or share icon in the top right to save your result.

iStyles can't access my camera or photo library.

Open the iOS Settings app, scroll to iStyles, and make sure Camera and Photos access are turned on. If you denied access when first prompted, this is the only way to re-enable it.

My edited photo won't save or share.

Make sure you have enough free storage on your device and that Photos access is allowed for iStyles. Restarting the app after granting a new permission usually resolves this.

I have a question about a purchase or subscription.

Email us with the approximate date and, if you have it, your App Store order number. We can look into billing questions or help you restore a purchase on a new device.

I'd like to suggest a new filter or feature.

We read every suggestion — email us a short description of the idea and we'll consider it for a future update.

How do I report a bug?

Email us what happened and the checklist below so we can reproduce it quickly.

Please include if you can:

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings → General → About)
  • The iStyles app version (visible on the app's settings screen)
  • What you were doing right before the issue happened
  • A screenshot, if the issue is visual
